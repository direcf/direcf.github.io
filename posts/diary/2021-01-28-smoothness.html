Smoothness

Be smoother. Be patient.

I've read Mark Twain's Adventures of Huckleberry Finn. The character Finn decided to pretend to believe what the liars said in the narrow raft. He thought, "happiness at the moment may be more important than the right answer." I also think like that. The thought like this helps me become smoother.
